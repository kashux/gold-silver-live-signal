📖 Plain-English glossary
😨 VIX
A number that shows how nervous or calm the stock market is feeling right now. Higher = more fear, lower = more calm.
🌡️ Risk regime
A simple label (Low, Below-average, Above-average, High) for how nervous the market is, based on VIX.
📐 r² (r-squared)
A score from 0–100% showing how much one thing (like VIX) actually explains another (like gold's price move). Low r² = it barely explains it.
🎲 p-value
Tells us whether a pattern is probably real or just random luck. Smaller usually means "more likely real."
🏃 Momentum
Whether a price has been trending up or down over the last few weeks, and by how much.
🔁 Mean-reversion
The idea that when something is unusually high or low vs. normal, it tends to drift back toward normal — eventually, but not on any fixed schedule.
⚖️ Gold/Silver ratio
How many ounces of silver it takes to equal the price of one ounce of gold. A simple way to compare the two.
🔍 Confidence
How trustworthy a pattern is, based on real historical data. Low confidence = the pattern is real but weak or unreliable.
🧪 Backtest
Testing an idea against real data from the past to see whether it would actually have worked.
